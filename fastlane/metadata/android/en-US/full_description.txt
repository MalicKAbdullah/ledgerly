Ledgerly turns the admin side of freelancing into a few taps: send a polished invoice, record a payment, track an expense and always know where you stand. Everything is encrypted on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

INVOICES THAT LOOK THE PART
- Auto-numbered invoices with line items, tax and discounts.
- Three professional PDF templates with your logo and a PAID stamp.
- Share as PDF straight from the app.

GET PAID, STAY ON TOP
- Record full or partial payments and track the balance due.
- Estimates and quotes that convert to an invoice in one tap.
- Recurring invoices that generate themselves on schedule.

KNOW YOUR NUMBERS
- Track expenses and see real profit, not just revenue.
- A dashboard with outstanding, overdue and paid-this-month at a glance.
- Client statements and CSV export.

MONEY DONE RIGHT
- Amounts are stored as exact integer minor units (cents) with their currency, so totals always add up.

YOURS ALONE
- Optional biometric app lock.
- All data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a key held in the Android keystore.
- Encrypted backup and restore: a passphrase-protected file you control.

PERMISSIONS, IN PLAIN WORDS
- Notifications: to show an on-device reminder when invoices are overdue or about to fall due. Nothing leaves the device.
- Biometrics: only for the optional app lock.
- Ledgerly does not ask for contacts, location or microphone. The store build declares no network permission.
